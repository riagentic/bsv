# Job — cautions for the source pass (binding with BRIEF.md and OT.md)

- Poetry (3:3–42:6): one Hebrew colon per printed line, in the Hebrew order; model `10-2Sam/2Sam22.md`. Prose frame 1–2 and 42:7–17.
- Speech headings: וַיַּעַן X וַיֹּאמַר "And X answered and said" — one formula through the book.
- Divine names: אֱלוֹהַּ `eloah` (very frequent), שַׁדַּי `shaddai`, אֵל `el`, אֱלֹהִים `elohim`, יהוה `yhwh` (frame, 12:9, 38–42). Never level one to another; never add one.
- הַשָּׂטָן (1–2): "the adversary" / „protivník“ (reviewer row), Changes: *ha-satan*, traditional "Satan".
- בֵּרַךְ written for "curse" (1:5, 11; 2:5, 9): translated as written ("bless"), explained in Changes (row as 1 Kgs 21:10).
- Qere always in the verse, ketiv in Changes: 13:15 qere לוֹ "for him" (ketiv לֹא "not"), and every other qere orig.ts prints.
- 19:25–27; 14:14; 33:23: word for word, no interpretation in the verse (גֹּאֵל has its row).
- Job 4:6; 20:23; 21:17; 24:23 were fixed to the MT before (tools/terms/lex-todo.md): keep the MT reading.
- Constellations 9:9; 38:31–32: עָשׁ / עַיִשׁ, כְּסִיל, כִּימָה, מַזָּרוֹת — tokens where a key exists, else plain word, uncertain in Changes.
- Rare words are many: plainest sense, "uncertain" in Changes, one rendering per word. Look up first (`words.ts --word`), add the row when you are the first, and run words.ts on your chapters again before you finish: 12 other workers add Job rows at the same time.
- Animals 38–41 (בְּהֵמוֹת, לִוְיָתָן): existing tokens; unknown animals plainest sense, doubt in Changes.

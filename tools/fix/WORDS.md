# Source pass — fixed renderings (binding)

One original word → one rendering, across all books, wherever the sense is the
same. Read this before a chapter. If you need a word that is not here and it
will recur, append a line (`>>`, never rewrite the file) and use it. If a line
here is wrong for a verse (different sense), keep the verse right and say why in
Changes.

## Standing decisions

- A neuter relative or pronoun for πνεῦμα is rendered "which" / "it" in EN and
  the gender of „Duch“ in CZ; add a Changes note that the gender is grammatical.
- A supplied object or subject (e.g. "him" after ἀρνέομαι) is allowed only when
  the English or Czech sentence cannot stand without it, and is always noted.
- An unfinished Greek sentence stays unfinished (dash), with a Changes note.
- The oath formula (Hebrew אִם, Greek εἰ: "if they shall enter …", Heb 3:11; 4:3,
  5; Ps 95:11) means a sworn "they shall not". The verse gives that meaning
  ("They shall not enter into my rest"); Changes gives the literal form. A
  word-for-word "if" would tell the reader the opposite of what is said.
- A genitive of relation without a noun (ὁ τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου, Σίμων Ἰωάννου): supply
  "son(s)" only where the text itself names the relation elsewhere (Mark 10:35,
  John 1:42), with a Changes note. Where the relation is not known (John 19:25
  Μαρία ἡ τοῦ Κλωπᾶ) supply nothing: "the one of Klopas".

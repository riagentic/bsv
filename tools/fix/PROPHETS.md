# Prophets, Lamentations, Daniel — cautions for the source pass (binding with BRIEF.md and OT.md)

- Poetry: one Hebrew colon per printed line, Hebrew order (model `10-2Sam/2Sam22.md`); prose stays prose. Follow the accents.
- Qere always in the verse, ketiv in Changes. Nothing from the Septuagint, a scroll (1QIsa) or a parallel book goes into a verse; readings from versions in Changes, marked "from memory".
- Run `words.ts` on your chapters again before finishing: other workers add rows at the same time. Look up a word (`--word`) before adding a row.
- Formulas, one wording each through the prophets (the first worker that meets one adds the row; look it up first): נְאֻם יהוה "declares ⟦yhwh⟧" · כֹּה אָמַר יהוה "Thus says ⟦yhwh⟧" · וַיְהִי דְבַר־יהוה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר "And the word of ⟦yhwh⟧ came to me, saying" · מַשָּׂא "burden" (Changes: "oracle"; the word is "what is lifted up") · הוֹי "Woe" / „Běda“ · בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" · יהוה צְבָאוֹת "⟦yhwh⟧ of Hosts" (plain "of Hosts", capital H, as the earlier books; no token).
- שׁוּב שְׁבוּת "restore the fortunes" / „obnovit úděl“ (reviewer row 2026-10-16).
- Verses quoted in the NT: translate the Hebrew as it stands; never level it to the NT quotation (or the reverse); the difference in Changes. Isa 7:14 עַלְמָה "young woman" (not "virgin": בְּתוּלָה is "virgin"; the NT quotation follows the Greek παρθένος: Changes); Isa 9:6 (Heb 9:5) the names word for word; Isa 52:13–53:12; 61:1–2; Jer 31:31–34; Hos 11:1; Joel 2:28–32 (Heb 3:1–5); Amos 9:11–12; Mic 5:2 (Heb 5:1); Hab 2:4; Zech 9:9; 12:10 (qere/MT "to me whom they pierced"); Mal 3:1; 4:5–6 (Heb 3:23–24).
- Hebrew numbering differs in places (Isa 9; Jer 9; Ezek 21; Dan 4; Hos 12–14; Joel 2–3; Jon 1–2; Mic 5; Mal 3–4): orig.ts shows `(Heb x:y)`; keep our numbers, name the Hebrew ones in Notes.
